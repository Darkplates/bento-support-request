# Google Ads — documented case overview

**As of 23 October 2026.** This account draws on original email records, screenshots, a preserved Google Ask Advisor transcript and selected local work histories. Source references lead to redacted public evidence notes; the corresponding originals and precise locators are retained privately.

## The restriction and its personal scope

Google's 10 October appeal refusal states:

> When a Google Ads account is suspended, that suspension applies to the account owner and any current or future linked accounts.

> If you create additional Google Ads accounts while you’re suspended, the new accounts will get suspended immediately.

> Account suspensions are permanent. You can’t run ads unless your account is reactivated.

These are the notice's words, with line breaks normalised. The restriction has no fixed expiry and continues unless reinstatement occurs. It reaches my use of Google Ads and linked accounts across my activities. It is not evidence that my YouTube channel, Gmail or every Google service has been banned. [E02](evidence/02_google_appeal.md)

My principal commercial priority is consulting with AI agents and automation. Games, children's animation through Tiny Big Worlds, and other Bento Corp products also need customers and audiences. I had used paid promotion for Tiny Big Worlds; the restriction also obstructs my plans to advertise the other activities. I am not claiming that all those ventures had already bought ads or earned revenue. [Projects](PROJECTS.md)

## The advertisement I did not authorise

**I did not create or authorise this advertisement.** That is my direct testimony about the Russian-language sexual-supplement advertisement in Display-1, whose external destination was unrelated to my work. Original screenshots show its content, destination and disapproved state inside a removed campaign. They establish the advertisement's presence; they do not identify the person who created it. [E09](evidence/09_historical_advertisement.md)

My position is that the advertisement resulted from unauthorised access in 2021. The preserved transcript of Google's own Ask Advisor describes unauthorised activity, gives 10 April 2021 at 18:50:39 UTC as the campaign's creation time, and says the account was suspended on 11 April at 09:15:55 UTC with no impressions, clicks or charges. Google's AI-generated support emails separately give 30 March 2021 as the account creation date. These are specific statements originating from Google's products and correspondence; they deserve a response against the underlying records. They are not an authenticated export of historical access logs or a signed human security finding. [E09](evidence/09_historical_advertisement.md)

The technical intrusion and the causal link to the October 2026 suspension are separate questions. The material reviewed supports a substantive investigation into unauthorised historical activity. It does not independently establish the access mechanism, the intruder's identity, or that this advertisement was the cause of the later cloaking decision. A broken URL or a content disapproval is not, by itself, proof of cloaking.

## Questions the correspondence has not resolved

1. Which specific advertisement, destination, account link or conduct supported the October cloaking decision, and when did it occur?
2. What do the original records establish about account creation, campaign creation, authorisation, access and notifications in 2021?
3. What historical period and evidence were examined in the security investigation? How does its conclusion relate to the account of unauthorised activity given by Ask Advisor?
4. Which stages involved manual assessment or automated processing? What happened to the specialist review announced on 14 October?
5. What correction route is available for the historical material, and what are the final outcomes of the advertisement appeals shown as pending on 18 October?

The manual-detection and automated-process replies may describe different stages. They create an unresolved ambiguity, not proof that every appeal was decided solely by a bot. Similarly, a 19-minute email interval establishes timing, not the method or adequacy of the underlying review.

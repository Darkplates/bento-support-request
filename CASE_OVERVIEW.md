# Google Ads — case overview

Prepared on 23 October 2026 from selected emails and the account holder's documentary archive. This is a public account of a disputed suspension, not a legal judgment or forensic finding.

## Why this matters to my work

My main professional priority is digital consulting using AI agents and automation. I am also developing games and producing children's animation. I used Google Ads to promote Tiny Big Worlds, but my concern reaches across all the ventures I am building under Bento Corp. The suspension has consumed time and resources that I need for creative and commercial work. I report spending nearly 200 hours on appeals and documentation over about two weeks; this is my account, not an independently audited time log.

**The Google notice says the suspension applies to the account owner and any current or future linked accounts. It also says suspensions are permanent and advertising cannot resume unless the account is reactivated.** That is the central problem: it restricts my ability to use Google Ads across the businesses associated with me, including my planned AI consulting activity. It is not merely the rejection of one video or campaign. The notice does not establish a ban on my YouTube channel or a deliberate restriction of its organic reach.

## What I dispute and want clarified

I dispute responsibility for the alleged violation. I believe it is connected to a historical Russian-language advertisement placed during a suspected unauthorised intrusion in 2021, around five years before this suspension. I do not recognise the advertisement as my work. The precise history, attribution and causal link to the 2026 suspension have not been established by the records published here; I want those facts investigated and the historical logs examined.

I am seeking a clear explanation of the specific advertisement, destination and conduct underlying the suspension, a meaningful review of the evidence, and a workable route to correction or reinstatement if the suspension is unjustified. I also want clarity about the outcome of the specialist review and the scope of the security investigation.

## Limits of the evidence

- ANACOM's referral is a documented procedural development. It is not a finding that Google broke the law or an order to reinstate the account.
- ADR's update does not confirm a final admissibility decision or a decision on the merits.
- Google has provided signed support replies and expressly stated that detection was manual. This brief does not claim that every decision was made solely by a bot.
- The payment total is gross money paid into advertising, not a proven refund entitlement or damages figure. Advertising was delivered, and a current reconciled balance is needed to establish any refund amount.
- The effect on future growth is my assessment. This brief does not establish a particular lost-profit figure or prove that success would otherwise have been guaranteed.
- No final resolution is established by the selected records. This is a dated selection, not a complete export of the mailbox or every procedural development.

## Assistance sought

Practical guidance, appropriate professional introductions, and consideration of financial support that could help me continue my work. The separate request to verify possible ancestry and citizenship eligibility is a personal enquiry; the Google Ads documents do not establish either.

The last receipt precedes the 9 October suspension notice by 9 hours, 11 minutes and 17 seconds. That establishes sequence, not a reason for the suspension.

The total records gross payments applied to Google Ads. Delivered promotion is visible in [E15](15_business_impact.md). The full USD 1,460 cannot be characterised as an unused balance, money spent on the disputed historical advertisement, an established loss, or a refund entitlement. The approximately USD 507 balance reported in earlier correspondence has not been reconciled to a current billing statement in this audit.

Account identifiers, payment-profile identifiers, personal details and links are withheld. Different receipt dates are retained because the date applied and Lisbon email date are not always the same.

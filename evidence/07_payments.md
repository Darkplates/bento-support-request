## Meaning and omissions

These confirmations establish gross payments applied to Google Ads. They do not establish that the whole amount is refundable, unused or recoverable as damages. Some funds paid for advertising already delivered. A current account statement and reconciliation are needed to establish any remaining balance.

Account identifiers, payments-profile identifiers, recipient details, tracking links and mail-routing information are omitted. Original records remain with the account holder.

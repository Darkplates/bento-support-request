# E12 — Manual detection and automated suspension descriptions

**Sources:** three Google support emails, each read directly from the mailbox. Translations below are unofficial; original Portuguese passages are retained privately.

| Date and Lisbon time | What support said |
|---|---|
| 9 October 2026, 19:24:05 | The violation was identified manually. |
| 15 October, 16:08:15 | The representative would like to reactivate access manually, but describes the process as automated and directs the advertiser to provide evidence of compliance. |
| 19 October, 14:53:42 | The account suspension is an automated platform process; the route offered is evidence and a transparent history in an appeal. |

The 19 October reply also says WhatsApp is unavailable and that instability in the calling system prevents calls at that time.

These later communications were missing from the earlier public version. They materially qualify any simple description of the process as manual. However, detection, enforcement and appeal review can be distinct stages. The messages do not explain that distinction or establish that every decision was made solely through automation.

The concrete request is for Google to explain which stages used automation, what human review occurred, what evidence was examined and how the conclusions were reached. The correspondence does not establish an unlawful automated decision by itself.

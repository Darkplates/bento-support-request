## Verification and publication choices

E01–E04 were checked against the received emails on 23 October. E08 uses a previously retrieved email record. E05–E07 use identified pages of the account holder's existing CEC documentary archive. Each file distinguishes the source text from an English summary and states omissions.

Routing headers, account and case identifiers, tracking links, staff names and unrelated personal data are withheld. No family records, identity documents, private source code or confidential legal consultations are published here. The account holder retains the originals and can discuss a suitable verification process with a legitimate recipient.

Portuguese excerpts retain the sender's original language, including Brazilian Portuguese where used by support staff. English summaries are unofficial. Formatting and line breaks may be normalised for readability.

The public brief was assembled with AI assistance from material selected and checked for this request. It remains the account holder's presentation of a disputed case, not an independent investigation. The full mailbox and every attachment have not been audited for this publication.

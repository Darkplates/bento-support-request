## How to read the evidence

- **Documented observation:** what an original email or image actually states or shows. A provider's statement is not automatically proof that its underlying conclusion is correct.
- **Direct testimony:** Pedro's account of what he did or did not authorise, and his experience and plans.
- **Inference:** a conclusion connecting records, explicitly distinguished from a provider-confirmed cause.
- **Unresolved:** matters requiring original technical records, clarification or later procedural outcomes.

English originals are quoted as such. Portuguese messages are summarised or translated into English and identified as unofficial translations. Original wording, including any provider qualifications, is preserved privately. Dates follow Lisbon time unless UTC is expressly specified.

All eight payment messages and the support/regulatory messages used in these notes were checked directly in the connected mailbox during this audit. Selected screenshots were read as images; OCR and AI histories were used to locate sources, not to substitute for them. The Ask Advisor transcript is Google's in-product AI material, not a Claude/Gemini summary, and is identified accordingly.

Account, campaign, ad, payment-profile and private case identifiers, personal contact details, access links and unrelated third-party information are omitted from this public package. Original images and raw correspondence are retained privately. No ancestry records or identity documents are included.

This is a substantial, targeted documentary review, not a complete export or examination of every disk, mailbox attachment or conversation. It does not independently establish technical compromise, the exact suspension trigger, legal liability, damages or a guaranteed business outcome. The detailed coverage and remaining gaps are recorded in the private audit.

# E01 — ANACOM referral to Ireland

**Source:** received ANACOM email, 20 October 2026, 14:22:22 Lisbon time, checked in the connected mailbox. English translation and summary of the relevant Portuguese passages; identifiers and unrelated details omitted.

ANACOM states that it considers the submitted evidence sufficient to justify investigation of the facts and intervention within its Digital Services Act role. It confirms that the complaint was transmitted to Coimisiún na Meán, Ireland's Digital Services Coordinator, through the European Commission's AGORA information-sharing system.

ANACOM says it will contact the complainant when the AGORA case is updated and asks him to preserve the information collected. It also expressly distinguishes regulatory supervision from resolving an individual dispute, pointing to courts or certified out-of-court dispute-settlement bodies for the latter.

**What this establishes:** a substantive preliminary regulatory response and a confirmed referral. It is stronger than a mere automatic acknowledgement.

**What it does not establish:** a finding that Google violated the law, acceptance of every factual allegation, an order to reinstate the account, or an award of compensation. The email refers to a communication dated 12 October; a separate sent record proves an earlier complaint on 10 October. Those dates should not be conflated.

**Sources:** original account screenshots dated 9–10 October 2026; a preserved nine-page Ask Advisor transcript identified in the correspondence as the 21 September transcript; Google support emails of 2 October; Pedro's sent request of 3 October. Original source paths, hashes and precise locators are retained in the private audit.

## What the images show

Display-1 appears as a removed Display campaign. The advertisement contains Russian-language sexual-supplement text and an external destination unrelated to Pedro's children's animation or consulting work. The ad is shown as ineligible/disapproved. The original URL is retained privately for verification; it is not an access link readers need to visit.

These images establish the advertisement's content and presence in the account at the time of capture. A foreign-language advertisement does not establish an intruder's nationality. The displayed zero metrics concern selected October 2026 date windows; those screenshots alone do not prove zero lifetime delivery in 2021.

## Pedro's direct testimony

> I did not create or authorise this advertisement.

The 3 October sent email already disputes conscious creation/use of the account and campaign in 2021 and asks for the original account, access, notification and authorisation records. It also expressly recognises that Ask Advisor is AI-generated and asks for human confirmation.

## What Google's own AI material says

On page 4 of the preserved transcript, Ask Advisor presents its answer as details from the account records. It gives:

- Campaign creation: **10 April 2021, 18:50:39 UTC**.
- Account suspension for payment fraud: **11 April 2021, 09:15:55 UTC**.
- **Zero impressions, zero clicks and USD 0.00 charged**.

It describes the activity as unauthorised. Pages 4–5 also discuss a possible session-hijacking mechanism and assert that notices were sent in April 2021. The generic mechanism explanation is not a case-specific forensic finding. The supposed notification delivery has not been established by message identifiers or dispatch records.

Two Google support emails of 2 October state **30 March 2021** as the account creation date. Both identify themselves as “powered by Google AI” and warn that information about the account may be inaccurate. Neither should be described as a signed human confirmation of account history or compromise.

This material comes from Google's own product and support correspondence, which makes it directly relevant to what Google should reconcile. It is distinct from later Claude or Gemini summaries. Nevertheless, the transcript is a preserved conversation, not an independently authenticated export of backend records. Exact historical dates and technical conclusions require that confirmation.

## Conclusion

The advertisement's existence, its mismatch with Pedro's work, his unequivocal denial of authorisation and the specific account given by Ask Advisor support investigation of unauthorised historical activity. The reviewed material does not independently resolve the technical intrusion or prove its causal relationship to the 9 October cloaking suspension. Google's adverse security conclusion must also be addressed. [E06](06_google_security_reply.md)

No claim of malware, stolen cookies, a national origin for an intruder, or a confirmed Google security failure is made here.

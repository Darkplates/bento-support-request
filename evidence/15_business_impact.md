# E15 — Advertising use and business impact

**Sources:** original YouTube Studio promotion and analytics screenshots in the account holder's evidence archive; payment receipts in [E07](07_payments.md); Pedro's statement of professional priorities.

Promotion screenshots show children's animation associated with Tiny Big Worlds, expenditure and delivered impressions/views. Two selected video analytics screens attribute 99.3% and 98.9% of the displayed views to YouTube advertising. Those figures concern those videos and displayed reporting periods, not every channel view or subscriber.

They substantiate actual use of paid promotion and its significance to that activity. They do not prove deliberate suppression of organic reach, an account-wide lifetime percentage, or the revenue the channel would otherwise have earned. A subscriber-source category labelled “Other” cannot be relabelled as advertising without additional evidence.

The wider impact follows from the owner-and-linked-account scope of the notice: Pedro's plans to advertise AI consulting, games and other Bento Corp services are also affected. The reviewed screenshots do not claim those activities had already run campaigns. Planned consulting is his main commercial priority, while Tiny Big Worlds is the documented example of prior advertising use.

Pedro reports spending around 200 hours dealing with the dispute. Numerous dated messages, work histories and document versions substantiate sustained work; they do not provide an audited timesheet. This brief makes no quantified lost-profit or compensation claim.

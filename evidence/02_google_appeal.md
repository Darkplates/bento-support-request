# E02 — Google rejects an appeal

Sender: Google Ads. Date: 10 October 2026, 07:34:07 UTC. Subject: update on account suspension appeal. Source: original received email, re-read on 23 October.

## English summary

Google says the appeal was unsuccessful under “Circumventing systems: Cloaking” and the account remains suspended. The notice states that suspension extends to the account owner and linked accounts. It describes another appeal and conditional refund options.

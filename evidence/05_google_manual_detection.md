# E05 — Google describes detection as manual

**Source:** Google Ads support email received on 9 October 2026 at 19:24:05 Lisbon time, freshly checked in the mailbox as well as in the local archive.

The reply attributes the suspension to cloaking and states, in Portuguese, that Google uses automatic and manual assessments and that this violation was identified manually. An unofficial English translation of the decisive sentence is:

> This violation was identified manually.

The message also says suspension is permanent unless an appeal succeeds and describes conditional refund options.

The statement must be retained when evaluating how the decision was made. It does not identify the human reviewer, reviewed evidence, specific advertisement or destination. Later support replies call the suspension process automated; [E12](12_manual_and_automated_processes.md) presents those replies alongside this one.

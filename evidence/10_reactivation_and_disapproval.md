**Sources:** original Google emails and an original policy-history screenshot, checked against the mailbox and local files.

| Event | Lisbon time |
|---|---|

The policy-history screenshot describes the earlier appeal under “Suspicious payment activity” and shows it as “Complete”. Approval is separately established by the email; “Complete” alone would not prove success.

The disapproval message names Display-1 and identifies “Destination not working” and “Non-family safe”, with HTTP 404 on desktop. The private campaign identifier ties this notice to the campaign visible in the account screenshots.

The disapproval follows reactivation by **1:54:34**, and completed identity verification by **0:19:38**. This is a concrete documented connection between the historical campaign and a 2026 policy notification. It does not establish that a crawler's reassessment caused the 9 October suspension, or that Google approved or exonerated every historical advertisement by reactivating the account.
